-----------------------

Deployment StrategyType: RollingUpdate

Max Unavailble: 25%
   Deployment ensures that only a certain number of Pods are down while they are being updated, by default it ensures that atleast 75% of the desired pods are up ( 25% max unavailable)

Max Surge:
  Deployment ensures that only certain number of pods are created above the desired number of pods.By default it ensure that atleast 125% of the desired number of pods are up (25% max surge)
