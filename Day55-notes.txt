Persitent Storage

Persistent Volume (PV) : PV is the logical storage resource such as AWS EBS Volume, Azure Disk, or NFS Share , Local disk , or HostPath .

    2 types of PV provising:
	------------------
	1. Static PV - Kubernetes Admin or Storage Admin he will create the PV
	2. Dynamic PV --> Dynamic PV or PVC are created dynaically with the help of StorageClass

Persistent Volume lifecycle is indepedent to the POD workload utilized by it.


Even after deleteing the Workloads( PODS, Deployment) volume object should exists (PV,PVC)


Persistent volume will be provisioned by Adminstator statically or dynamically by StorageClass

2. Block (RAW Storage)
