Kubernetes Deployments(Deploy)
---------------------------------

Deployments
Services
Job & Cronjobs
Auto cleanup for finished jobs
DaemonSet
StatefulSet
Probes (startup, readiness,liveness probes)

1.Deployment objects has the same feature as ReplicaSets has provided.

2. Deployment in addition it has rollout(upgrade) and rollback(downgrade) applications

3. workflow - Deployment--> ReplicaSet --> POD

4. Features: Rollout,Rollback , Scalein , Scaleout , Pause Rollout/Rollback , remove older RS

5. apiVersion  for Deployment object - apps/v1

6. when Deployments manages Relicaset, it created the pod-template-hash label will be created and we need to make sure that it should not be modified

7. Rollout and Rollback can be performed through Imperative(recommended) or Declarative approach

8. When we do rollout , the deployment  will maintain the number of rollouts and you can get the information about rollout by executing the commands


kubectl rollout history deployment <deployment-name>

kubectl rollout undo deployment/deploy-name

kubectl rollout undo deployment/deploy-name --to-revision=3

9. Duplicate revision details (podTemplate) are not stored, for which latesth revision number will be listed and old one will be removed 

10. Deployment supports 2 strategies :
    1. RollingUpdate (default) 
	     Max Unavlable & Max Surge
	2. Recreate

11. Deployment revision history limit is default 10 , and it can be increased by setting under .spec.revisionHistoryLimit: 20
